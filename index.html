Welcome to ACHARYA SHIV ACADEMY

A learning space for students studying under CBSE and BSEB, based in Darbhanga, Bihar.

Our aim is to help students develop clear concepts, confidence in learning and good study habits. Lessons can include explanation, practice questions, revision and guidance suited to the student's class level.
